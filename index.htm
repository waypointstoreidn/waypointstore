How to Order

Simple & Easy Guide to Order Supplies Directly to Your Vessel

1

Select Products

Browse through various categories, tap to view product details, and click + Add to Cart for the items you wish to purchase.

2

Fill In Details

Scroll to cart section, enter your full Crew Name and Vessel Name accurately.

3

Choose Payment

Select your preferred method: BNI Rupiah, BNI Dollar, or Cash on Board Please ensure your order totals at least 50 USD (per vessel) so that we can process it.

4

Confirm & Send (WA)

Click Proceed and send your order via WhatsApp. For bank transfer, attach your payment receipt photo!
